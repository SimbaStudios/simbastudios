Forever Lost VR

Wake up in places that feel abandoned, but not empty. Explore unfamiliar rooms, solve environmental puzzles, follow distant sounds, and piece together why the building seems to know you.

About the game

Forever Lost VR is about being somewhere wrong and noticing the details. Search forgotten offices and service halls, inspect objects and notes, use old terminals, and find your way through spaces that should not connect. The tension comes from small changes and the feeling that something is just out of sight.

In development

The game is still in progress. Rooms, puzzles, and other parts of the experience may change as development continues.
