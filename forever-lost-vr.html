Forever Lost VR

A VR horror game set in a neighborhood that feels familiar at first. Stay a while, and things start to feel off.

I want it to feel like an old 3D game you might have found on a shelf in the late '90s. Quiet streets, ordinary houses, and something not quite right once you step inside. You're there to look around and figure out what happened.

Networking

I'm building the multiplayer side with Simba Networking, my own networking project for Unity. It's the bit that lets players connect and share a game session.

Still being made

I'm still working out the spaces and how the game should feel to play. There is more to do; I'll add details here as it takes shape.
