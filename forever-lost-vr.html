Forever Lost VR

A horror game for VR about places that seem normal until you spend a little too long in them.

I'm aiming for the feel of an old late-90s 3D game: quiet neighborhoods, empty houses, and rooms with the sort of furniture you recognize. You explore, look for clues, and try to work out what changed while you weren't looking.

Still being made

I'm still figuring out the rooms and how the game plays. I'll add more here as it comes together.
