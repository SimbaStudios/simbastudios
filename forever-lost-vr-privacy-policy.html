Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 30, 2026

At Simba Studios, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your information. This Privacy Policy explains what information Forever Lost VR may collect, why it is collected, and how it is used.

By using Forever Lost VR, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

Information We Collect

To provide online features and keep the game running smoothly, Forever Lost VR may collect certain information while you play.

This may include:

  • Your IP address
  • Basic device information, such as your device model and operating system
  • Your Meta account identifier used for authentication
  • Your in-game username or display name
  • Player progression and save data
  • Inventory and cosmetic ownership
  • Multiplayer session information
  • General gameplay statistics and analytics
  • Crash reports and diagnostic information that help identify bugs and improve stability

We collect only the information that is reasonably necessary to operate, maintain, secure, and improve the game.

Why We Collect This Information

The information we collect helps us:

  • Allow players to connect to online multiplayer services
  • Save your progress and game data
  • Verify ownership of in-game content and purchases
  • Improve game performance and stability
  • Identify and fix bugs
  • Protect the game from cheating, fraud, and abuse
  • Maintain the security and reliability of our online services
  • Better understand how players use the game so we can continue improving it

Network Information

Like most online multiplayer games, Forever Lost VR receives your IP address when you connect to our servers.

Your IP address is used for normal network communication, allowing you to connect to our online services and multiplayer features. It may also be used to help protect the game from spam, malicious activity, unauthorized access, cheating, or other behavior that could negatively affect other players.

We do not collect or use your IP address for advertising purposes, and we do not sell your personal information.

How We Protect Your Information

We take reasonable steps to protect the information we collect. We use security measures designed to help prevent unauthorized access, misuse, loss, or disclosure of player information.

While no online service can guarantee absolute security, we continually work to improve the safety, reliability, and security of our systems.

Sharing Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

We may share limited information only when necessary to:

  • Provide online services
  • Verify purchases or account ownership
  • Comply with applicable laws or legal obligations
  • Respond to valid legal requests
  • Protect the rights, safety, and security of our players, community, or services

Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as it is reasonably necessary to:

  • Operate and maintain the game
  • Save player progress and account information
  • Provide customer support
  • Improve our services
  • Protect the integrity and security of our systems
  • Meet applicable legal or regulatory obligations

When information is no longer needed, we will take reasonable steps to securely delete or anonymize it where appropriate.

Your Data

You may request that your personal information or account data be deleted at any time by contacting Simba Studios using the contact information provided below.

After receiving and verifying your request, we will make reasonable efforts to delete information that we are no longer required to retain for legal, security, or operational purposes.

Please note that requesting the deletion of your information does not remove, reset, or overturn enforcement actions taken against your account. If your account has been suspended or permanently banned for violating our Terms of Service, Community Guidelines, or other applicable policies, requesting data deletion cannot be used to bypass, avoid, or reset those actions.

To protect the integrity of our services, we may retain limited information when necessary to:

  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Protect the security and integrity of our services
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, ban evasion, or other malicious activity
  • Enforce our Terms of Service and other policies
  • Resolve disputes or investigate security incidents

Children's Privacy

Forever Lost VR is not intended for children who are below the minimum age required to use the platform on which the game is available.

If we become aware that we have unintentionally collected personal information from a child in violation of applicable law, we will take appropriate steps to remove that information.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

As Forever Lost VR continues to grow and improve, this Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time.

When changes are made, the updated version will be posted with a revised Effective Date. We encourage players to review this Privacy Policy periodically to stay informed about how their information is handled.
